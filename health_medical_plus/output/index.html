What is a CAC test, and should you get one?

A coronary artery calcium, or CAC, test is a quick, noninvasive CT scan that looks for calcium deposits in the coronary arteries. Calcium can be an early sign of plaque buildup, or atherosclerosis. The resulting "calcium score" can give individuals and their health care professionals another piece of information about future risk of cardiovascular disease or an event such as a heart attack or stroke.
