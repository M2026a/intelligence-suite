Are you sleep deprived? Your spit may hold answer

Sleep loss dulls alertness and coordination, and it can produce effects similar to severe intoxication, making actions like driving incredibly risky. But there's no clinical test for determining when someone is dangerously sleep deprived. Now, researchers report a step toward a non-invasive test for sleep deprivation in the Journal of Proteome Research. In a study of 20 men, they identified molecular differences in saliva after a full night's rest and 24 hours without sleep.

睡眠不足ですか？あなたの唾には答えが含まれているかもしれない

睡眠不足は注意力や協調性を鈍らせ、重度の酩酊と同様の影響を引き起こす可能性があり、運転などの行為が非常に危険になります。しかし、人がいつ危険なほど睡眠不足に陥っているかを判断するための臨床検査はありません。今回、研究者らはJournal of Proteome Researchで睡眠不足の非侵襲的検査に向けた一歩を報告した。 20人の男性を対象とした研究では、一晩休んだ後と24時間眠らなかった後の唾液の分子の違いが特定されました。

Deep South HIV prevention care has gaps where patients need it most

A recent University of Mississippi study has identified hundreds of counties in the South where HIV prevention and health care may not be keeping pace with the local needs, leaving communities without resources to address the persistent problem. The research, published in Frontiers in Public Health, offers health care and government leaders a new tool for targeting HIV-related support where the need is highest.
