Deep learning model using ECGs during sleep studies can predict cardiovascular outcomes

A research team supported by the National Institutes of Health (NIH) has found that electrocardiograms (ECGs) administered during sleep and paired with sleep stage information can be used to determine the risk of future adverse cardiac events. The findings were published in the journal SLEEP.
