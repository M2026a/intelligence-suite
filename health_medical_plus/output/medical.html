FDA approval offers a new endocrine therapy option for people with early-stage breast cancer

About 70% of breast cancers are hormone receptor-positive, meaning the cancer cells have receptors that allow hormones such as estrogen to promote their growth. For people with early-stage hormone receptor (HR)-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer, surgery is often followed by endocrine therapy to reduce the chance that the cancer will return. Depending on the patient, treatment can include tamoxifen or an aromatase inhibitor, often taken for five years or longer.
