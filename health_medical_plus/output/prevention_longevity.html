One in six hospital beds filled by dementia patients

The study is the most comprehensive review of dementia hospitalizations ever undertaken in the UK. The researchers outline how delayed discharges, lack of communication among NHS staff and hospital environments unsuitable for dementia patients are keeping people in hospital unnecessarily, eroding dignity and exacerbating frailty. Those with dementia are five times more likely to remain in hospital despite being medically fit to leave, with delayed discharges alone costing £328 million annually to taxpayers.

病院のベッドの 6 分の 1 が認知症患者で埋まっています

この研究は、英国でこれまでに行われた認知症入院に関する最も包括的な調査である。研究者らは、退院の遅れ、NHS職員間のコミュニケーション不足、認知症患者に不適当な病院環境がいかに人々を不必要に入院させ、尊厳を蝕み、虚弱性を悪化させているかを概説している。認知症患者は医学的に退院可能であるにもかかわらず入院を続ける可能性が5倍高く、退院の遅れだけで納税者に年間3億2,800万ポンドの損害が発生している。

Researchers analyzing data from more than 214,000 people found that dementia risk factors differ widely across countries, challenging the idea of a one-size-fits-all prevention strategy. A hidden Alzheimer’s tipping point may decide who gets dementia

Scientists have identified a possible tipping point that helps determine whether Alzheimer’s-related brain changes lead to dementia. The key may lie in how the brain’s immune cells respond to plaques and tau, offering a promising new target for treatments designed to extend cognitive resilience.

APOE2 may protect the brain from Alzheimer’s and aging

The longevity-linked APOE2 gene appears to protect brain cells by reducing DNA damage and helping neurons recover from stress. The finding could open a new path toward treatments that mimic APOE2’s defenses in people at higher genetic risk for Alzheimer’s.
