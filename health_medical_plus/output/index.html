Daily step count of remote workers associated with lower stress and better work performance

A study by researchers at the University of Tsukuba published in the American Journal of Health Promotion found that higher daily step counts were associated with improved work performance through reduced stress responses in Japanese workers with remote work arrangements. The findings suggest that increasing walking and overall physical activity may be a practical strategy for reducing stress and enhancing performance among remote workers.

Kidney cancer gene reveals an unexpected weakness and a new path for treatment

A common genetic mutation that helps some kidney cancers survive may also expose an unexpected weakness, one that MUSC Hollings Cancer Center researchers hope to transform into a new treatment strategy. In a study published in Cancer Research, Aguirre de Cubas, Ph.D., and colleagues have discovered that kidney cancer cells lacking the tumor suppressor gene SETD2 become highly dependent on a protein called BCL-xL for survival. By targeting that dependency, the researchers were able to selectively eliminate SETD2-deficient cancer cells in laboratory models while largely sparing cancer cells with intact SETD2. The findings identify a potential new therapeutic strategy for patients with a particularly aggressive subset of kidney cancers.

腎臓がん遺伝子により予期せぬ弱点と新たな治療法が明らかに

一部の腎がんの生存を助ける一般的な遺伝子変異は、予期せぬ弱点を露呈する可能性もあり、MUSC ホリングスがんセンターの研究者らは、それを新たな治療戦略に変えたいと考えている。 Cancer Research に掲載された研究で、Aguirre de Cubas 博士らは、腫瘍抑制遺伝子 SETD2 を欠く腎臓がん細胞は、生存のために BCL-xL と呼ばれるタンパク質に大きく依存することを発見しました。その依存性をターゲットにすることで、研究者らは実験室モデルでSETD2欠損がん細胞を選択的に除去すると同時に、無傷のSETD2を持つがん細胞をほとんど残すことに成功した。この研究結果は、特に悪性度の高い腎癌のサブセットを患う患者に対する潜在的な新しい治療戦略を特定するものである。

Tick-borne diseases reported every month of the year and in every Illinois county

A new analysis of state health department data reveals that more than 6,400 Illinois residents were diagnosed with tick-borne diseases from 2004 to 2022. The most commonly reported infections in the state are Lyme disease, occurring across Illinois but at higher rates in northern counties; anaplasmosis, reported most often in northern and southern counties; ehrlichiosis, seen most often in southern Illinois; and spotted-fever group rickettsioses, serious and sometimes deadly infections that also are more prevalent in southern Illinois.

州保健局のデータを新たに分析したところ、2004年から2022年までに6,400人以上のイリノイ州住民がダニ媒介性疾患と診断されたことが明らかになった。州内で最も多く報告されている感染症はライム病で、イリノイ州全域で発生しているが、北部の郡で発生率が高い。アナプラズマ症は北部と南部の郡で最も頻繁に報告されています。エーリキア症、イリノイ州南部で最も頻繁に見られます。もう一つは紅斑熱型リケッチア症で、これもイリノイ州南部で流行している重篤で時には致死的な感染症です。
