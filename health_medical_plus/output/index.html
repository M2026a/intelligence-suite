For many years, traditional Medicare has operated almost entirely without prior authorization, a requirement that clinicians obtain approval before certain services will be covered. The practice is controversial because it adds paperwork, outside review and other barriers that can delay or deny coverage for recommended treatment. While prior authorization is widely used by commercial insurers and Medicare Advantage, traditional Medicare has historically almost never required it.

Guideline on shorter-course radiation therapy for localized prostate cancer updated

An updated clinical guideline from the American Society for Radiation Oncology (ASTRO) provides evidence-based recommendations on the use of hypofractionated radiation therapy for patients with localized prostate cancer. The guideline replaces ASTRO's 2018 recommendations and reflects a matured evidence base showing that shorter-course radiation therapy can maintain cancer control while reducing overall treatment burden for patients receiving definitive external beam radiation therapy (EBRT). The guideline was published in Practical Radiation Oncology.
