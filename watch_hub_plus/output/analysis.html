Monster Hunter Outlanders Video Review

Monster Hunter Outlanders reviewed by Hayes Madsen, played on iOS (iPhone 14 Plus), also available on Android."Monster Hunter Outlanders is simply one of those games that has too much stuff; it's overflowing with mobile-style fluff to keep track of, much of which gets in the way of enjoying that strong gameplay formula it clearly has. In many ways, it's genuinely remarkable how ambitious Outlanders is, and how deftly it manages to adapt the brilliance of Monster Hunter World into a smaller package. The smart streamlining of combat still keeps the intensity of hunts alive, while being more playable in short bursts. And while there's enough good here to keep you engaged, it equally comes with no shortage of frustrations in monetization, roadblocks, and the headache of endless menu management. That means, ultimately, Outlanders doesn't reach the heights of its console counterparts, but if you can go in with tempered expectations, the heart of Monster Hunter is still there."
