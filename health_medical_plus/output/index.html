Unit of Health: Report explores wearables data as longevity indicators

Wearable devices and health apps are making it increasingly possible to translate daily choices into estimates of future health. A new Gerontological Society of America (GSA) report, "Unit of Health: The Currency of Longevity," illustrates how a person's longevity can be explored using daily habits as recorded by wearable devices and in electronic health records. The proposed "Unit of Health," or Unit•H, imagines one added day of healthy life as a practical benchmark. The report cautions, however, that giving people a precise personal forecast could mislead or distress users and deepen health inequities.

How radiation or chemotherapy before bone marrow transplantation may shape human immune system recovery

In a new study from the USC Stem Cell lab of Rong Lu, Ph.D., researchers used genetic barcoding to track individual human blood-forming stem and progenitor cells after transplanting them into mice. The study revealed how different types of human blood-forming cells regenerate the blood and immune system, and how different forms of pretransplant conditioning, a treatment used to prepare a recipient before bone marrow transplantation, affected blood and immune cell recovery.
